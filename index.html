Applied Research — Axion Deep Labs R&D

PERSIST Preprint
topology-based forgetting prediction
A 57-experiment study (19 architectures × 3 datasets) applying persistent homology to loss-landscape cross-sections to predict catastrophic forgetting. Key result: H0 fragmentation predicts EWC regularization benefit (ρ = 0.76 to 0.86).
  • Rigorous stats: LOAO ridge regression, permutation tests, clustered bootstrap, Bonferroni correction; ImageNet-100 scale validation on A100 HPC
  • Preprint published on SSRN; NSF SBIR Phase I application ($275K) in progress
