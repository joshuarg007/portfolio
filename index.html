PERSIST Research
topology-based prediction of catastrophic forgetting
A 57-configuration study (19 architectures across 3 datasets) applying persistent homology to loss-landscape cross-sections to predict how much a model will forget, and how much regularization will help.
  • Key result: H0 fragmentation predicts EWC benefit, ρ = 0.86 on RESISC-45 and 0.76 on CIFAR-100, and replicates on 2 of 3 datasets. The third, CUB-200, does not reach significance and is reported as such
  • Statistics stated with their limits: leave-one-architecture-out ridge regression, permutation tests, clustered bootstrap, Bonferroni correction. Borderline p-values are labelled borderline, and the EWC-benefit finding is flagged as exploratory rather than pre-registered
  • Scale validation on ImageNet-100 across 8 configurations on A100 HPC, where H1 rather than H0 becomes dominant (ρ = 0.93)
  • Code public under MIT; preprint in preparation

EMERGE Pre-registered
when in-context learning emerges
Open study of the task-diversity threshold at which in-context learning appears in from-scratch transformers, using in-context noisy linear regression with exact closed-form baselines for in-weights and in-context behavior.
  • Research plan pre-registered and frozen before data collection; every deviation recorded as a written amendment rather than a silent edit
  • Phase 0 pilot complete at 28/28 cells. Phase 0B measure validation passed all five pre-set criteria, but only after a failed first run exposed a wrong premise in one criterion, which was diagnosed and amended in public
  • Built for zero legal exposure by design: synthetic data from seeded RNGs, models trained from scratch, no third-party weights or datasets, MIT from the first commit

Topological Dynamics of Grokking Pilot complete
early-warning signals for delayed generalization
Does the persistent homology of loss-landscape slices give an early-warning signal before a model groks? A 1-layer transformer on modular addition, 100K steps, with the endpoint hierarchy fixed in advance.
  • Outcome: mixed, and reported as mixed. The pre-registered primary endpoint came out negative. Two secondary measures and the comparator came out positive. With only three pre-onset windows the result is inconclusive either way
  • Conclusion recorded as "motivates a larger study", not as a finding, and the endpoint hierarchy was frozen rather than promoting a secondary measure to primary after seeing the data
  • Two measurement bugs found and fixed during the work, including one where a statistic turned out to be a structural constant rather than a signal
